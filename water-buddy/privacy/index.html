Water Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Water Buddy ("the app", "we") is a water tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. In short: your water log stays on your device, and we do not run any servers that receive it.

Data stored only on your device

The following never leaves your phone unless you back up your device with Apple or Google:

  • Water and other drinks you log (amounts, times and the type of drink), daily totals and streaks
  • Alcoholic drinks you note in the alcohol log (type, amount and time)
  • Your daily goal, and the weight and activity level you enter to calculate it (only the resulting goal is stored)
  • Reminder schedule, cup sizes, and Buddy's name, color and outfit
  • Whether you finished onboarding, and whether the app has already asked you for a rating

The home screen widget reads today's total from the same storage on your device.

We cannot see, access or recover this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions, and single outfits you buy once, are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details.

To check whether you have an active subscription or have bought an outfit, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription service provider. RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID (not linked to your name, email or phone number);
  • your purchase and subscription history from the App Store or Google Play;
  • basic device and app information needed to validate purchases (for example, app version, platform, country/store).

This data is used only to provide and restore your subscription and your outfits. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple Health

On iPhone you can turn on Apple Health in Settings. Only then, and only after you allow it in Apple's Health permission window, the app saves the drinks you log to Health as Water. It asks for no permission to read your Health data; it only updates or removes the Water entries it saved itself, when you change or delete a drink in the app. Health data is not sent to us or to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not stored in iCloud by us. You can turn it off in Settings or in the Health app at any time.

Backup to a file

"Back up" in Settings saves your log and settings in one file and opens your phone's share sheet, so you choose where it goes (for example Files, a cloud drive or email). "Restore" reads such a file back. We never receive it.

Sharing your streak

"Share" on your streak makes a picture card on your device and opens the share sheet. It goes only where you send it.

Sending a postcard

"Send a 'Drink water!' postcard" in Settings makes a picture card on your device, with the name you type if you want one, and opens the share sheet with that picture and a line of text. It goes only where you send it. Nothing goes to us, and the name you type is not saved.

Tapping your bottle

An NFC sticker on a bottle can hold a link that starts with waterbuddy://. When you touch your phone to it, your phone opens the link in the app, and the app adds one glass to the log on this phone. The app does not read NFC itself, asks for no NFC permission, and the link only carries an amount: it contacts no server.

Quick settings tile (Android)

If you add Buddy's tile to the quick settings panel, it shows today's total from the storage on your device and adds a glass when you tap it. It needs no permission and sends nothing.

Exporting your history

With Pro, you can export your daily totals as a CSV file. The app creates the file on your device and opens your phone's share sheet; the file goes only where you choose to send it (for example email, Files or a cloud drive). We never receive it. Whoever you share it with handles it under their own terms.

Rating the app

Once, after you have met your goal on three days, the app may show the App Store or Google Play rating window. That window is run by Apple or Google: a rating or review you leave there is handled by them under their policies, and we see it only as a public review on the store. "Rate Water Buddy" in Settings opens the same window or the store page.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads and no advertising identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No tracking across other apps or websites

Children

Water Buddy is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • You can turn reminders off in Settings at any time.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • To ask about the purchase data held by RevenueCat, or to request its deletion, contact us at the address below.

Health information

Water Buddy offers general hydration guidance. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
